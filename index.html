The problem

Your buyers moved to AI answers. Your brand isn't in them.

Answer engines reward content that's structured, specific, and constantly updated. Regulated brands can't ship that fast — every sentence goes through legal, medical, or compliance review — so third-party sources speak for you instead, and sometimes get it wrong.

How it works

Review once. Compose freely. Get cited.

The operating model contract teams already proved — a clause library plus composition rules — applied to regulated marketing content.

1

Claim & Block Library

Every claim with its substantiation, approved phrasings, and expiry — plus content blocks legal signs off exactly once.

2

Governed Composer

Drag-and-drop, but rule-enforced: efficacy claims auto-pair with safety language; expired claims refuse to render. AI drafts inside the system — compliant by construction.

3

Exception-Only Review

Approved blocks publish with no queue. Only genuinely novel language routes to legal — with a full, immutable audit trail.

4

Cited

Schema-ready answer blocks built for the engines. Track citation lift and review-cycle compression in one place.
