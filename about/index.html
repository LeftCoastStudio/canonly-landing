Why does this product exist?

The agency's regulated clients asked the same question in different words: how do we show up in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews when every sentence we publish needs legal, medical or compliance sign-off? The standard AEO advice (publish more, faster, as questions and answers, and keep it fresh) is correct and, for those clients, close to useless, because their review queue was built for quarterly campaigns. The fix the agency kept rebuilding by hand was the same each time: inventory the claims legal already approved, turn them into reusable blocks with their disclosures attached, compose answer pages from the blocks, and route only new language back for review. Canonly is that fix as software. The Compliance-Approved AEO Playbook is the written version of the same work.

What does the design-partner program involve?

Canonly is onboarding a small group of regulated brands as design partners. Each partner gets hands-on onboarding of its first claim library and answer blocks, direct access to the founder, and a measurement baseline taken before anything ships. The program measures two things: citation lift across a prompt panel of real buyer questions, and review-cycle compression (review rounds per asset, days to approval, and the share of content shipped with zero review cycles). Partners shape the product roadmap and are the first reference customers. Request early access with the form below, or email jason@leftcoastagency.com.

How does the agency background shape the product?

Coast Studio's work sits where paid acquisition meets regulation: HIPAA-compliant tracking for telehealth, Meta's health and wellness advertising restrictions, FTC health-claim substantiation, and platform policy for fintech and edtech. That work produced two habits that carry into Canonly. The first is treating compliance as an input to the build, because a gate at the end is where the delays and the rewrites live. The second is measuring in two directions at once: the outcome the marketer cares about and the review burden the compliance team carries. Canonly reports citation lift and review-cycle compression side by side for the same reason.

Which industries does Canonly serve?

  • Pharma and health: MLR review and OPDP rules, with ISI pairing and label-scoped claims. See MLR review and AI search.
  • Banking and fintech: UDAAP and FTC Act Section 5, with rate and fee disclosures as required pairings.
  • Insurance: state advertising regulations, with state-scoped approved contexts.
  • Wealth and advisory: the SEC Marketing Rule and FINRA Rule 2210. See FINRA 2210 in AI answers.
