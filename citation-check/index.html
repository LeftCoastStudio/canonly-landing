Why do regulated brands score low?

Answer engines reward content that is specific, structured and current. Regulated brands send every sentence through legal, medical or compliance review, so they publish less, and third-party sites answer buyer questions on their behalf. Canonly fixes the operating model: legal approves claims and content blocks once, marketing composes from them freely, and the engines cite what legal already approved. The full argument is in how regulated brands get cited in AI answers.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check whether AI answers cite my brand?

Ask each answer engine the questions your buyers ask about your category, then record whether your brand is cited as a source, mentioned without a link, or absent. Canonly's free Citation Check generates ten category questions with one-click links for ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode, then scores the results.

Why are regulated brands missing from AI answers?

Answer engines favor content that is specific, structured and current. Regulated brands route every sentence through legal, medical or compliance review, so they publish less, and third-party sites answer buyer questions on their behalf.

Does Canonly see my answers?

No. The check runs in your browser and your own answer-engine sessions. Canonly receives your score only if you submit the form.

Why check three engines?

ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Mode retrieve sources differently, and a brand can lead in one while it is absent from the others.
